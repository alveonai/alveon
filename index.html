How AI video generation works

AI video is generated as a unified whole — scenes, shots, and transitions are inseparable. Individual elements (a line of text, a single frame, a small detail) cannot be changed within an already-generated video. Each generation is also unique: even with identical inputs, no two outputs will be the same.

What to expect from revisions

Revisions are not edits — they are new generations. If changes are needed after delivery, a new version is produced from scratch based on updated or more precise direction. The result will be different from the original, but guided toward your requested adjustments.
